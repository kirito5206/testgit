## Low-level intervals (diagnostic, NOT VAD ground truth)

An FFmpeg `silencedetect=noise=-38dB:d=0.55` pass over the audio track logged:
- **891** qualifying low-level intervals;
- **1,088.72 seconds** aggregate (approximately **43.65%** of duration);
- Median interval approximately **1.0 s**, maximum approximately **6.48 s**.

This identifies portions whose measured amplitude stays below a threshold; it does **not** mean that 43.65% contains no speech. Quiet words, expressive whispering, and musical backgrounds must not be discarded solely by this metric.

Six 8-second spot checks at 0, 120, 600, 1250, 2000 and 2480 seconds had RMS values spanning approximately -41.8 to -29.7 dBFS, and peaks ranging approximately -22.2 to -12.1 dBFS. Those are spot checks, **not** whole-track loudness statistics.
